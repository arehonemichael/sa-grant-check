Privacy Policy

SA Grant Check – R370 & R350

Effective date: 26 August 2026

Independent app notice: SA Grant Check is an independent information app, not affiliated with or endorsed by SASSA. All applications, status checks, and appeals are completed on SASSA's official website.

1. Information we collect

SA Grant Check does not ask for, collect, store, cache, or transmit your South African ID number, SRD application cellphone number, banking details, passwords, PINs, one-time passwords, or other grant application credentials.

The app does not require an account or sign-in.

2. Information stored on your device

The app may store simple local preferences on your device, such as whether onboarding has been completed, your light/dark mode preference, and article bookmarks. This information stays on your device and is used only to make the app easier to use.

3. Official SASSA services

When you choose to check your status, apply, request reconsideration, or lodge/check an appeal, the app opens the relevant SASSA webpage in a browser. Any personal information you enter there is handled by that website, not by SA Grant Check.

4. External links and contact services

The app includes links to official SASSA pages, WhatsApp, and the SASSA call centre. When you use those services, their own privacy terms and practices apply.

5. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

This version of SA Grant Check does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, or third-party tracking tools.

6. Device permissions

The app is designed to use a minimal permission footprint. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, SMS messages, or call logs.

7. Children

SA Grant Check is a general information app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

8. Data security

Because the app does not collect personal grant credentials or maintain a user database, those details are not stored by us. Users should only enter grant-related personal information on the official SASSA website.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated before that version is released.

10. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the support contact shown on the Google Play Store listing.
