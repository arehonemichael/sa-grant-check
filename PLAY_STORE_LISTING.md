# Google Play listing draft

## Title
SA Grant Check – R370 & R350

## Short description
Independent SRD grant information, status guidance and SASSA website links.

## Full description

Unofficial SASSA SRD grant information and news app.

SA Grant Check is an independent information app, not affiliated with or endorsed by SASSA. All applications, status checks, and appeals are completed on SASSA's official website.

SA Grant Check – R370 & R350 helps South Africans understand common SRD status results, read simple grant guidance, save useful articles, and reach the correct SASSA web pages without recreating SASSA's forms inside the app.

What you can do:
- Open the SASSA SRD status-check page in a browser.
- Open the SASSA SRD application and reapplication website.
- Open the SASSA reconsideration page when that route applies.
- Open the SASSA / ITSAA appeal page.
- Understand common results such as Approved, Pending, Declined and Reconsideration.
- Read bundled grant news and guidance that remains available offline.
- Bookmark useful articles on your device.
- Use light mode, dark mode or your device appearance setting.
- Find SASSA WhatsApp and call-centre contact links.

Privacy:
SA Grant Check does not ask for, collect, store, cache or transmit your South African ID number, SRD cellphone number, banking information, OTPs or grant credentials. Personal application details are entered only after you choose to open SASSA's website.

Information sources:
- South African Social Security Agency: https://www.sassa.gov.za/
- SASSA SRD service: https://srd.sassa.gov.za/

Important:
This app does not submit or process applications, approve grants, issue payments, change application outcomes or guarantee payment dates. Any payment estimator or timing explainer in the app is clearly labelled as unofficial and must not be treated as a SASSA payment schedule.

Before publishing each release, verify government-related guidance and links against the current SASSA and SRD websites.
