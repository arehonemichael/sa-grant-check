# Google Play listing draft

## App name
SA Grant Check – R370 & R350

## Short description
Independent SRD grant news, status guidance and official-site links.

## Full description

Unofficial SASSA SRD grant information and news app.

SA Grant Check – R370 & R350 is an independent information resource that helps South Africans understand common SRD status results, follow grant-related updates, save useful articles, and find the correct official SASSA SRD pages.

SA Grant Check is an independent information app, not affiliated with or endorsed by SASSA. All applications and status checks are completed on SASSA's official website.

What you can do:
- Open the official SASSA SRD status-check page safely.
- Open the official SRD application/reapplication website.
- Understand common status results such as Approved, Pending, Declined and Reconsideration.
- Read grant news and guidance with offline caching.
- Bookmark useful articles.
- Use an clearly labelled unofficial payment-window estimator.
- Turn on optional local alerts when the app detects new remote-feed articles.
- Use dark mode.
- Find official SASSA contact and source links.

Privacy:
The app does not ask for, collect, store or transmit your South African ID number or the cellphone number used for an SRD application. Official application and status details are entered only on SASSA's website after you choose to open it.

Information sources:
- South African Social Security Agency: https://www.sassa.gov.za/
- Official SRD service: https://srd.sassa.gov.za/

Important:
This app does not process grant applications, approve grants, issue payments or guarantee payment dates. Any estimator shown in the app is clearly marked as unofficial and must not be treated as an official SASSA payment schedule.

Before publishing, verify all source links, contact information and grant guidance against current official sources.
