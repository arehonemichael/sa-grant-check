# Google Play Console answers — Version 1

Use this as a release checklist. Answer truthfully if the app changes before submission.

## App access

- Does the app require login, membership, a password, or special access? No.
- Reviewers can access all native app screens without an account.
- Official SASSA links open externally in a browser and are not recreated inside the app.

## Ads

- Contains ads: No.

## Data Safety

Current Version 1 design:

- Does the app collect or share required user data? No, based on the current app code and included SDKs.
- No ID numbers or SRD cellphone numbers are requested or stored.
- No banking information, OTPs, grant credentials, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location are collected.
- Local preferences such as onboarding completion, theme choice, and bookmark IDs stay on the device.

Before submission, re-check the final dependency list and generated Android permissions to ensure this remains accurate.

## Government apps declaration

- Is this app developed by or on behalf of a government entity? No.
- Is it officially affiliated with SASSA or the South African government? No.
- The listing and app clearly state that it is an independent information app.
- Government information sources are clearly shown in the app and store listing.

Sources:
- https://www.sassa.gov.za/
- https://srd.sassa.gov.za/

## News apps declaration

- The app includes a small local News section but its primary purpose is an SRD information utility and official-link guide, not a general news publication.
- If Play Console asks whether it is a News or Magazine app, assess the final app category and wording at submission time. Do not claim it is a news organization.

## Target audience

Suggested intended audience for this utility: adults / users eligible to interact with SRD services.

Do not select a child-directed audience unless the app is specifically redesigned for children and all Families requirements are met.

## Content rating

Complete the IARC questionnaire based on the final app. This app contains general public-service information and no user-generated content, gambling, violence, sexual content, or controlled-substance promotion.

## Financial features declaration

The app does not provide loans, banking, payments, money transfers, financial advice, cryptocurrency, insurance, investment services, or lending. It only provides information and outbound links related to a social grant.

## Permissions

The intended Android release should request only permissions generated by the Expo/native libraries actually needed. The app does not require contacts, camera, microphone, location, SMS, call logs, storage, accessibility, or broad file permissions.

## Privacy policy

A public privacy-policy URL is required in Play Console. `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` contains the draft text, but it still needs to be hosted at a publicly accessible URL before submission.

## Store graphics

Required / recommended release assets:

- Play Store icon: 512 x 512 PNG, max 1 MB
- Feature graphic: 1024 x 500 JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha
- At least 2 phone screenshots to publish
- Recommended: at least 4 portrait phone screenshots at 1080 x 1920 or higher for stronger Play Store presentation

Suggested screenshot order:
1. Home screen showing all six actions
2. Status action screen showing the independent-app disclaimer
3. Guide / status meanings screen
4. News screen or Settings/About screen showing sources and privacy wording

Screenshots must show the real current app UI.
