# Privacy Policy — SA Grant Check – R370 & R350

Effective date: 26 August 2026

SA Grant Check – R370 & R350 is an independent, unofficial information app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the South African Social Security Agency (SASSA) or the South African government.

## Information we do not collect

SA Grant Check does not ask for, collect, store, cache, transmit, sell, or share:

- South African ID numbers
- Cellphone numbers used for SRD applications
- Banking details
- One-time passwords (OTPs)
- SASSA passwords or login credentials
- Grant application information
- Precise location
- Contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, or other sensitive device data

All SRD applications, status checks, reconsiderations, and appeals are completed on SASSA's own website after the user chooses to open an official link.

## Local app data

The app may store limited non-sensitive preferences locally on the user's device, such as:

- Whether onboarding has been completed
- Light, dark, or system theme preference
- IDs of bookmarked news articles

This information stays on the user's device and is used only to provide the related app feature.

## External websites and services

The app contains links to official SASSA services and contact channels. When a user opens an external website or service, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply.

Primary information sources:

- South African Social Security Agency: https://www.sassa.gov.za/
- SASSA SRD service: https://srd.sassa.gov.za/

## Advertising and analytics

Version 1 of SA Grant Check does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or user-tracking services.

## Children

The app is a general information utility and is not specifically designed for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
